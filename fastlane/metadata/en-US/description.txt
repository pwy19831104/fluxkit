FluxKit saves media you are allowed to keep — HLS playlists, direct links, and podcast episodes — for offline playback on your iPhone.

WHAT IT SAVES
• HLS streams and .m3u8 playlists (video or audio)
• Direct files: MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, OGG
• Podcast feeds: paste an RSS or Atom feed and pick episodes
• Your own media: CDN links, S3/Cloudflare Stream URLs, self-hosted files, creator exports

HOW IT WORKS
1. Copy a link (or a whole share message).
2. Tap Paste, then Download.
3. FluxKit inspects the link, finds the media, and downloads it in the background.
4. Play it offline in the app, share it, or save video to Photos.

BUILT FOR OFFLINE
• Background downloads continue while the app is suspended
• Pause and resume picks up at the exact byte; HLS resumes from the last segment
• Podcast subscriptions, OPML import/export, and optional auto-download
• Queue with 1–4 concurrent downloads and a Wi-Fi-only rule
• Background audio with lock-screen controls, speed (0.75×–2×), and resume
• Library with search, sorting, multi-select delete, thumbnails and sizes
• Optional local notification when a background download finishes
• Share sheet and fluxkit:// deep links

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no cloud. Downloads go straight from the source to your device.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
FluxKit does not break DRM, bypass logins or paywalls, or extract media from platform-locked services. Links that only play inside another company's app (short-lived, signed playback URLs) are rejected with a clear explanation. Only download media you own or have permission to save.
